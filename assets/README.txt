GRAY365 STUDIO DIGITAL CARD

1. Centered header logo: add your logo image as assets/gray365-logo.png. A visible placeholder appears until you add it.
2. Profile photo: add your photo as assets/studio-profile.jpg. A placeholder appears beside “Gray365 Studio — Nail Technician & Nail Artist” until added.
3. Social profiles: in script.js, replace the empty Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat values with your actual profile URLs. Their logos are included as SVG icons.
4. Save Contact downloads a .vcf contact card with the studio name, phone number, website, WhatsApp and configured social profile links. Customers still need to open the downloaded file and confirm saving it. Contact apps may display social links differently.
